ABOUT ENTERPRET

At Enterpret, we are building the customer intelligence platform that turns millions of raw, messy pieces of customer feedback into a structured, queryable source of truth, solving complex problems in natural language processing, serverless computing, and real-time analytics by applying first-principle thinking to systems that operate at massive scale.

We are AI-native to the core. Our models, pipelines, and infrastructure are designed around AI from the ground up, and our engineers ship these features to production at scale. Our investors are Kleiner Perkins and Sequoia Capital, and a few of our customers are Canva, Notion, and Wispr Flow, among many other customer-centric product orgs.

We love working with folks who are resourceful, curious, and take ownership of their work. Our internship program isn't about side projects, it's a launchpad. You'll take on meaningful problems, learn quickly, and directly impact the systems our customers rely on every day.

Our engineering culture is built around leveraging and contributing to open-source tools. We aim to build upon and improve state-of-the-art systems in our field of work.

Read more about our team, core values, and operating principles: https://www.enterpret.com/careers

WHAT YOU'LL DO

Our backend team is small but exceptionally strong, so you'll learn fast and get real ownership early, with experienced engineers alongside you the whole way. You'll get to build things from scratch and see how your systems hold up in the real world, with all its messy data, unexpected user behavior, and edge cases.

And when something breaks (it happens to all of us), you'll dig in with the team to make it faster and more resilient than before.

- Join our system design discussions: sit in on internal design and implementation conversations and contribute your ideas.
- Take ideas from concept to production: build real solutions with guidance, not just closing tickets off a backlog.
- Develop on serverless architectures using AWS, building scalable backend components that stand up to real-world use.
- Help build proof-of-concepts for new backend + AI feature integrations, from model output to real-time inference.
- Work closely with the engineers building our AI-native platform and learn how backend architecture supports AI features end to end.

WHAT IT TAKES

- Onsite in Bangalore is a must-have: you're able and willing to work from our Bangalore office in person.
- Pursuing a degree in Computer Science or Engineering from a Tier 1 college.
- Strong programming fundamentals in any modern language.
- At least one prior internship in a relevant engineering or backend/AI role.
- Genuine interest in distributed systems, serverless computing, and AI-powered product features.
- Sharp problem-solving, debugging, and analytical skills, and an eagerness to learn how backend architecture supports AI from model training to real-time inference.
- Bonus: open-source contributions or backend/AI project experience.
